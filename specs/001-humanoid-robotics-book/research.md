# Research: Humanoid-Robotics-Book

**Date**: 2025-12-07
**Feature**: [Humanoid-Robotics-Book](./spec.md)

## Objective

To document the technology choices and architectural decisions for the comprehensive Humanoid Robotics book.

## Findings

The book's content generation and presentation rely on a core set of technologies and methodologies.

### 1. Content Generation and Formatting

-   **Platform**: Docusaurus will be the primary platform for hosting the book, leveraging its Markdown capabilities.
-   **Format**: All content will be written in Markdown, adhering to Docusaurus compatibility standards.

### 2. Core Robotics Technologies Covered

The book will delve into several key robotics frameworks and simulation tools:
-   **ROS 2**: As the foundational middleware for robotic systems.
-   **Gazebo**: For physics-based simulation of humanoid robots.
-   **Unity**: For advanced visualization, human-robot interaction (HRI), and potentially other simulation aspects of digital twins.
-   **NVIDIA Isaac**: Encompassing Isaac Sim for photorealistic simulation and synthetic data generation, and Isaac ROS for hardware-accelerated perception and navigation.

### 3. AI and Control Concepts

-   **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)**: A key paradigm for intelligent robot control, integrating perception (vision), natural language understanding, and robot action generation.
-   **OpenAI Whisper**: For robust speech-to-text transcription as part of the VLA pipeline.
-   **Nav2**: For advanced path planning and navigation capabilities, especially relevant for bipedal humanoid movement.

## Conclusion

The technology stack for the book's content is diverse and covers the essential tools and concepts in modern humanoid robotics and AI. The focus is on leveraging these technologies for practical application and understanding.
